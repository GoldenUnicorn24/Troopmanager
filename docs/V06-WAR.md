# v0.6 War implementation

The existing resumable `BattleEngine` retains its three fronts, reserve, phases, events, commander rescue, cavalry sortie, pursuit and ordered retreat. v0.6 extends those exchanges; it does not replace the previous simulation or create per-soldier objects.

## Implemented behavior

- Pre-battle formations can be moved by long-press drag and drop into left, center, right or reserve. Screenreader-accessible buttons provide the same moves. Commanders move with their assigned contingent, so they cannot command duplicated formations.
- Nine terrain types modify real combat power. Hills aid ranged troops; forest, mud and narrow crossings penalize cavalry; woodland cultures receive a forest advantage. World encounters supply their actual terrain, season and weather. Expedition deployments are strictly limited to the units and commander present at that map location. Their enemy fronts face the occupied expedition fronts, allowing a single-command infantry force to fight without artificial empty-flank defeat.
- Visual formations project the live aggregate army into at most 300 groups. Their represented soldier counts sum exactly to the player and enemy forces. Position, casualty, routing, reserve, cavalry, artillery and the player's hero marker follow the persisted session.
- Orders consume command points. Leadership, tactics, active commanders, morale and battle chaos affect regeneration. Holding costs zero so a resource shortage cannot trap an event. Proactive orders use the same exchange and decision pipeline as event orders.
- Morale has euphoric, stable, nervous, wavering, panicked and routing states. Losses, hunger, experience, broken neighbouring fronts, leadership and commander injury affect morale. Routed troops remain living survivors in their real army pool but contribute no combat power. Unlocked rally can bring them back.
- Personal participation increases deployed morale and direct-command strength. The hero perk increases this effect. Participation can cause injury or capture; commander battlefield death is possible only with the explicit permadeath setting. A fatal player outcome additionally requires dynasty mode; it immediately runs succession, including the council fallback for childless rulers. Without dynasty, the optional fatal player submode remains disabled and severe injuries/capture are recoverable. Injuries, unconsciousness, capture and death persist and prevent commander deployment; rescued commanders recover sooner.
- Existing defensive siege devices include ladders, rams, towers, catapults, climbers and tunnels. Tunnels bypass reinforced gates. Real attacking siege orders consume timber/iron and command points, lower enemy fortification and weaken its protection. Artillery can target devices or enemy fortification.
- Siege preparation stores food, reinforces gates and evacuates civilians. Food transfers from the protected depot exactly once when defensive combat starts. Evacuation reduces civilian siege casualties. Wall damage creates persistent building damage and lowers effective building output; repairs debit gold, wood and stone.
- Final combat losses are separated into dead, wounded, missing and captured. Wounded and own captives remain citizens outside all combat pools. Hospital/physician care increases survival and shortens recovery; recovered soldiers return exactly once without creating additional people. Own prisoners return through exchange. Foreign prisoners can be released, exchanged, ransomed from the actual payer faction treasury, or accepted after a waiting period and diplomatic requirement.
- Arsenal stock is aggregated by unit type. Emergency wood equipment restores heavily damaged home formations at the cost of improvised quality; later arsenal investment can rebuild normal or higher-quality gear. Production consumes timber and iron; stock repairs equipment of home troops. Repairs consume stock only when they produce a whole equipment point in the aggregate pool; fractional home shares never waste materials. Higher arsenal levels unlock good, masterwork and legendary quality, which change combat strength. Arsenal level also reduces battle wear.
- Named elite units refer to portions of existing pools rather than adding soldiers. Every pool loss reduces their members proportionally. Battles, victories, banner, traits and a bounded service history persist. Veteran victories can confer a meaningful discipline bonus.
- All compact battle summaries remain archived; the latest 24 retain their detailed replay context: place, commanders, start/result strengths, losses, turning points, seed and every accepted input. Each new battle snapshots the compact initial combat context. Replay calls the same `BattleEngine.advance` with those inputs, supports step-by-step and timed playback, and never writes rewards into the campaign. It preserves actual enemy faction identity and roster instead of using a generic monster composition.

## Integration and save compatibility

`GameState.war` owns new campaign data. Additional `BattleSession` fields all have defaults, so old active battles remain loadable. Legacy sessions that lack the initial replay context retain their report but cannot retroactively produce a deterministic replay. New snapshots include player/commander skills, equipment, morale, terrain, weather, siege reinforcement and unlocked battle perks. `WarEngine.validate` checks domain bounds and military population conservation. `WarEngine.tick` is day-idempotent and skips active combat.

Battle finish synchronizes world armies through `WorldEngine.reconcileBattle`. World-army reservations, deployed troops and survivors refer to the same aggregate pools. Missing troops are currently treated as a permanent population loss; they do not silently respawn.

## Verification

`V06WarTest` exercises terrain, order spending and starvation safety, routing, large-army group limits, hospital recovery conservation, persistent siege damage/repair, deterministic save-and-replay, personal participation, unlocked tactical perks, prisoner exchange and funded ransom, arsenal, elite identity and siege preparation. Existing `BattleEngineTest` and `V045BattleTest` remain regression coverage. Build/test/lint execution is owned by the central integration task; no verification result is claimed here before that run.

## Presentation limits

Battles remain stylized 2D aggregate formations rather than action combat. Injuries are resolved through combat events and post-battle risks, not individual hitbox simulation. The archive is deliberately bounded to protect offline saves. Named units use aggregate banners and service records; individual soldiers have no inventories or simulation agents.
